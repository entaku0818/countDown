Enjoy the wait for the days you're looking forward to.

Weddings, trips, concerts, birthdays, anniversaries, due dates —
CountDown shows "how many days left?" at a glance, made for people who love looking forward to something.

WIDGETS THAT BUILD EXCITEMENT
• See days left anytime with Home Screen widgets
• Lock Screen widgets supported
• Live Activity shows a real-time countdown as the day gets close

MAKE IT YOURS
• Set your own photo or a template image as the background
• Choose a style: days, progress bar or circle
• Show just days, or hours, minutes and seconds
• Repeat yearly, monthly or weekly — perfect for birthdays and anniversaries

LITTLE THINGS THAT MAKE WAITING FUN
• Write down what you're excited about in "Looking Forward" notes
• Reminder notifications the day before, a week before and more
• Past events are kept as "Memories"
• Share your countdown as an image on social media

PERFECT FOR
• Enjoying the run-up to your wedding
• Counting the days to a trip or honeymoon
• Fans who can't wait for the next concert
• Never forgetting birthdays and anniversaries
• Visualizing exams, graduation or a due date
